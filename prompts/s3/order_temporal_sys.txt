Provide an answer by repeating the pairs of triplets in the same order they were given and associating each of them to
their temporal relation tag. Answer in the form [((s1, r1, o1), (s2, r2, o2)) -> ...; ((s3, r3, o3); (s4, r4, o4)) -> ...; ...].
